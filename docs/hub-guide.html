Command Center guide

Start on Wall, then open the section that needs your attention.

Wall

Scan today’s timeline, open items, and System warnings. Open a tile for details.

Today

Check deadlines and upcoming events. Open Recap for the broader review. Use Done or Dismiss when the item calls for it.

Business

Check new leads and anything waiting on a reply. Use Marketing for recommendations and Business Pulse for market activity.

Local

Review nearby events and open the Local Events page for more detail.

Private

Open when you need personal status. Check the battery line if present. If the section is locked, use the hub’s unlock flow.

Work

Check deadlines, requests and pending replies when this tile is available. Prioritize the items that need your action.

System

Check routine failures, stale data and open problems. Green means checked, yellow means pending or unverified, and red means blocked or failed.
